# STAT170 Regression Analysis Project - UCR

Background

Students will work in groups of 4 to apply regression concepts and techniques learned in
class to real-world data using R. Your group will define a research question, select or
collect a data set, conduct an exploratory data analysis (EDA), fit and evaluate various
multiple regression models, and summarize your findings in a clear, well-organized
report and presentation. You are encouraged to use generative AI (such as ChatGPT) as
a critical thinking partner throughout this process. The goal is to learn how to supervise,
verify, and refine AI-generated suggestions using sound statistical reasoning- so you can
become a a reliable evaluator of AI output, not a passive user.

Project Goals

This project is designed to help you
• Strengthen your mastery of regression modeling, diagnostics, and interpretation.
• Develop effective prompt-engineering and AI-evaluation skills for applied data
analysis.
• Practice verifying, critiquing, and refine AI-generated ideas through statistical
logic.
• Collaborate effectively in data-driven inquiry and communicate your results clearly.

Project Components

• Research Design: Create a focused research question exploring relationships
among variables in your chosen topic.
• Data Acquisition: Obtain, clean, and prepare a dataset suitable for your research
question.
• Exploratory Data Analysis: Visualize and summarize your data to identify
patterns, trends, and anomalies.
• Model Development: Build, compare, and refine multiple regression models to
address your research question.
• Interpretation Reporting: Communicate your findings clearly in both a written
report and an oral presentation.
• AI Reflection Include brief documentation of how you used generative AI (when
applicable), how you evaluated its suggestions, and what you learned from
supervising its output.
